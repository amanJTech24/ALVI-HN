⚠️ Scientific & Analytical Governance Notice

Task Practice & Familiarity Effects: Repeated exposure to identical or similar cognitive tasks naturally leads to score improvements resulting from procedural test-retest learning, stimulus familiarity, and reduced anxiety. Such performance trajectories reflect task-specific learning and do not signify clinical stabilization or biological reversal of underlying neuropathology.

Acoustic Recording Variance: Acoustic parameters (fundamental frequency, zero-crossing rate, low-energy frame proportions) are influenced by microphone sensitivity, ambient room acoustics, speaking distance, and user fatigue. Observational shifts do not constitute medical evidence of disease progression.

Non-Diagnostic Research Tool: ALVI Pattern Explorer provides observational data for personal self-monitoring and cognitive engagement tracking. It does not calculate disease progression scores or replace clinical medical evaluation by licensed healthcare professionals.
